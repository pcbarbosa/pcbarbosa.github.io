Restore Purchases

Last updated: May 4, 2026

If you purchased TagaSalin Premium and do not see your premium access, you can restore purchases from inside the app.

How to Restore

  1. Open TagaSalin on the device where you want to restore Premium.
  2. Sign in with the same app account you use for TagaSalin, if sign-in is available.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Tap Get Premium.
  5. Tap Restore Purchases on the purchase screen.

Store Account

Purchases are restored through Apple App Store or Google Play. Make sure you are signed in to the Apple ID or Google account that originally purchased TagaSalin Premium.

Still Need Help?

If restore does not work, use the developer contact listed for TagaSalin in the app store listing, repository, or official support channel.
