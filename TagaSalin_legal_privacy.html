TagaSalin Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how TagaSalin collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app. It is designed to cover current features and future features that may be added to support language learning.

We aim to collect only what is needed to provide, secure, personalize, and improve the app.

2. Information We May Collect

Current app data may include your selected current language, target learning language, sign-in state, lesson progress, quiz results, settings, and other information stored on your device.

Future features may collect account details, profile name, avatar, email address, learning streaks, achievements, classroom or friend activity, feedback, support messages, purchase status, device information, diagnostics, analytics events, notification preferences, and cloud sync data.

If camera, media, or other device features are added, the app may ask for device permission before using camera, photos, files, or similar device features. Media will be handled according to the notice shown for that feature.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to run the app, remember your settings, show lessons in the right languages, generate quizzes, track progress, provide account features, sync data, prevent abuse, fix bugs, improve learning content, and respond to support requests.

If social, leaderboard, friend, or classroom features are added, limited profile and progress information may be shown to other users according to the settings and feature design.

4. Storage and Retention

Some information is stored locally on your device. If cloud sync, accounts, backups, leaderboards, classrooms, or subscriptions are added, some information may be stored on servers or with trusted service providers.

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the app, legal requirements, security, backups, and legitimate operational purposes. You may uninstall the app to remove local app data from your device, subject to your device behavior. You may request account and cloud progress deletion from Settings or by contacting support.

5. Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate the app, such as hosting, analytics, crash reporting, authentication, app store purchases, notifications, or customer support.

We may also share information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, to prevent abuse, or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of app assets.

6. Children and Students

TagaSalin may be useful to younger learners, but children should use the app with parent, guardian, or school permission where required.

If the app is directed to children under 13 or we learn that we collect personal information from children under 13, we will handle that information according to applicable children's privacy rules, including parental notice and consent where required.

Parents, guardians, or schools may contact patrickbarbosa5047@gmail.com to ask about access, correction, deletion, or limits on a child's information.

7. Your Choices

You can change your current language and target learning language in Settings. You can manage device permissions such as notifications in your device settings.

You can request account and cloud data deletion from Settings. You can also report a problem, suggest content improvements, or flag a quiz question from inside the app.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No app, device, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. International Use

If future services store or process data outside your region, information may be transferred to countries with different data protection laws. We will use reasonable safeguards where required.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as TagaSalin changes. If changes are material, we will try to provide notice in the app or through another reasonable method.

Continuing to use TagaSalin after the updated policy takes effect means you acknowledge the updated policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or account/data deletion requests, contact patrickbarbosa5047@gmail.com or use the support options in the app.
