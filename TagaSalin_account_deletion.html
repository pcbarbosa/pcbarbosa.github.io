TagaSalin Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your TagaSalin account and cloud progress data from inside the app or by email.

In the app

Open Settings, then choose Request Account Deletion. The app will show an in-app request form with your account details included so the request can be handled accurately.

By email

Send a request to the.atlas.project.hq@gmail.com or barbs.atlas@gmail.com from the email address used for your TagaSalin account.

Processing time

We will verify the request and complete deletion of app-owned account and cloud progress data within 30 days, unless a longer period is required for legal, security, fraud prevention, backup, or app store purchase record reasons.

What is deleted

  • Your TagaSalin account identifier and sign-in record.
  • Cloud-synced lesson progress, quiz completions, word mastery, hearts, and coins associated with your account.
  • App-owned Supabase profile and progress records associated with your account.

What may be retained

Some records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, app store purchase records, backups, or legitimate operational needs. Google Play and RevenueCat purchase records may be retained by those providers under their own policies.
