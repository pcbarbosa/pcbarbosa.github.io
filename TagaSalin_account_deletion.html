TagaSalin Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your TagaSalin account and cloud progress data from inside the app or by email.

In the app

Open Settings, then choose Request Account Deletion. Your email app will open with your account details included so the request can be handled accurately.

By email

Send a request to patrickbarbosa5047@gmail.com from the email address used for your TagaSalin account.

What is deleted

  • Your TagaSalin account identifier and sign-in record.
  • Cloud-synced lesson progress, quiz completions, word mastery, hearts, and coins associated with your account.

What may be retained

Some records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, app store purchase records, backups, or legitimate operational needs.
